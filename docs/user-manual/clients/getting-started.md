# Getting Started with Clients

Clients is where you manage every applicant/client profile and see their case history at a glance.

## Adding a client

1. From the sidebar, open **Clients** and click **Add Client**.
2. Fill in name, phone, email, address, passport number, expiry, and nationality — or use
   **Scan Passport** to skip the typing.
3. Save to add the client to your list.

### Scan Passport (AI OCR)

In the Add/Edit Client form, click **Scan Passport** to open the scanner. Drag-and-drop or upload
a photo of the passport's data page; Gemini Vision reads it and pre-fills first name, last name,
date of birth, nationality, passport number, expiry date, and gender. Review the extracted fields
before saving — you can correct anything the scan got wrong, or click **Continue with Manual
Entry** if the scan fails.

## Searching and browsing the list

Use the search box above the table to filter clients by name. The table shows each client's
contact details, visa status/active case (with a **VEVO** badge once a passport number and expiry
are on file), and date of birth.

## Case history

Click a client's row to expand it and see **Case History** — every case linked to that client,
with its current progress. Click **Visa Check** on a client's row to jump straight to the Visa
Advisor pre-filled for that client, or **Edit** to update their profile.

## Importing clients (CSV)

Click **Import** to bulk-add clients from a CSV file instead of entering them one by one.
