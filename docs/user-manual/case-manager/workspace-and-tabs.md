# Overview, the Workspace drawer, and Tabs

## The Overview tab

Opening a case lands you on the **Overview** tab — a one-screen summary of where the case is up to:

- **You are here** — the current task (the earliest task that isn't done yet), how many of the
  case's tasks are done, its due date and status. Click **Open task** to edit it.
- **Case info** — client (and applicant, if different), visa subclass and template, case owner,
  stage, next deadline (click it to jump to the Deadlines panel), and the date the case was opened.
  **Edit** opens the Edit case form. Change the stage from the **Stage** chip in the summary strip
  under the case title, as before.
- **Progress** — the last couple of completed tasks, the current task, and the next few coming up.
  Click any task to open it, or **View all** to open the Tasks tab.
- **Document checklist** — a donut showing how many of the case's documents are in overall, then
  one progress bar per checklist category showing how many items are verified, linked, pending, or
  waived. Click a category (or **Open**) to open the Document Checklist tab. If the case has no
  checklist yet, use **Generate one**.
- **Recent case files** — the five most recently uploaded files, plus an upload area. Click a file
  to preview it, or **View all files** to open the Case Files tab.

The left-hand Case Files rail is hidden on the Overview (the cards above cover it) and comes back
on every other tab, where you can still drag files onto checklist items.

## The Workspace drawer

A green **Workspace** button sits in the bottom-right corner of every case tab. Click it to open the
Workspace drawer on the right. It has:

- **View** menu — Overview, Tasks & deadlines, Notes, Case files, Document checklist, and (when the
  case has one) Eligibility assessment.
- **Tools** menu — Add task, Add deadline, Edit case, Document Checklist Generator, Document
  Compressor, Run Crusher (supported subclasses), 820 Bundle Builder (subclass 820 only), Chat with
  Edamame, Draft cover letter, and Re-check eligibility.
- **Suggested for** *the current task* — a few next steps, for example *Chase client* (when the
  current task is waiting on the client), *Request N missing documents*, *Verify N documents*,
  *Update current task*, *Create a task plan*, *Generate checklist*, or *Summarise case*. These are
  worked out from the case's tasks and checklist (not an AI call), and change as you move through
  tasks. Steps that need writing — an email to the client, a summary — open the **Agent** chat
  panel with a prompt already typed in; nothing is sent until you press send.

Both menus have a search box — start typing to filter, use the arrow keys to move, **Enter** to
open the highlighted item, and **Esc** to close. After you chat with the Agent, items related to
what you discussed are marked **AI PICK** and listed first in their group.

The drawer closes by itself after you pick something, or with **Esc** or the **×** button. Click the
**pin** icon to keep it open while you work — on a wide screen the page moves over to make room for
it. A pinned drawer stays open for the rest of your browser session; closing it with **×** unpins it.

## Tabs

A row of tabs under the case header is always visible: **Overview**, **Tasks**, **Case files**,
**Checklist**, and **Notes**. Tasks, Case files, and Checklist show a count. Click a tab to switch to
it. Choosing a View from the Workspace drawer switches to the matching tab.

Tools (Document Checklist Generator, Document Compressor, 820 Bundle Builder) open in their own
window over the case rather than as a tab. When you come back to a case, it reopens on the tab you
were last using.
