# Edie, the AI assistant

**Edie** is Edamame's AI assistant. Edie is on every page.

## Opening and closing Edie

- Click **Ask Edie** (the green button with the pod icon), or press **Ctrl+J** (**Cmd+J** on a Mac).
  On a case, the button is in the case header next to **Case actions**. On every other page it's at
  the right of the top bar.
- The panel opens down the right-hand side. On a wide screen the page, top bar included, moves over
  to make room, so nothing is hidden. On a narrow screen the panel slides over the page.
- Close it with the **✕**, **Esc**, or **Ctrl+J** again.
- Edie stays open or closed as you move between pages, and remembers your choice on your account,
  so it's the same on another computer.
- When Edie is closed and the case has suggestions, the **Ask Edie** button shows how many.

## On a case

Open a case and Edie becomes that case's assistant. The panel shows suggested next steps for the
current task, skills (Summarise case, Draft cover letter, Draft consultation checklist, Re-check
eligibility) and the case's chat. Edie already knows the client, the tasks and the checklist. Chats
on a case are saved with the case. See
[Overview, Edie, and tabs](../case-manager/workspace-and-tabs.md) for the details.

## On every other page

Everywhere else, ask Edie how to do something in Edamame (answered from this user manual) or a
general immigration question. The quick buttons are **How do I start a case?**, **How do Work Plans
work?**, **Check eligibility** (opens the Visa Advisor) and **Report a problem**. The two "How
do…" buttons ask Edie straight away. **Report a problem** starts a message in the chat box for you to
finish and send.

These general chats aren't tied to a case, so they're kept only until you close or refresh the
browser tab. To keep a conversation, have it on the case it's about.

## Reporting a problem or asking for a feature

In any Edie chat, describe something that doesn't work as documented, or a feature you'd like.
Edie checks whether it's already been reported and links the existing GitHub issue if so; otherwise
it drafts one for you to **Confirm** or **Cancel**. See
[Reporting a bug or requesting a feature](../case-manager/getting-started.md#reporting-a-bug-or-requesting-a-feature-from-the-chat)
for the limits, and note that filing posts to a **public** GitHub repository.
